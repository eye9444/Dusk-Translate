Cookie Policy

DuskTranslate does not intentionally use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or tracking pixels.

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. Current Storage Use

DuskTranslate uses browser storage that is necessary for features you request. We do not currently ask for analytics or marketing consent because those technologies are not installed.

TechnologyPurposeYour choice

2. Third-Party Requests

Supabase handles account sessions and cloud storage. Paddle processes checkout and customer-portal sessions for paid plans. Google OAuth redirects to Google when selected, and the page requests Google-hosted fonts. AI requests are sent directly to the provider you select only after you start a translation. These services may use their own storage technologies and receive network metadata needed to deliver their services; consult their policies.

3. Future Changes

Before adding analytics, advertising, or other non-essential storage, we will update this policy and implement consent controls where applicable.
