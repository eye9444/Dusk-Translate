STORIES, ACROSS LANGUAGES

A translation room built around the whole book.

DuskTranslate is a browser-based workspace for translating books and web novels. Japanese-to-English work remains its specialty, while focused routes into Vietnamese, Russian, German, and Spanish make the same chapter-first workflow useful to more translators.

WHY DUSKTRANSLATE

Translation is more than one prompt.

A novel project has chapters, recurring names, house terminology, revisions, unfinished passages, and a final book to assemble. DuskTranslate keeps those pieces together instead of treating every request as an isolated chat.

01

Bring the actual book

Import EPUB, TXT, JSON, or a DuskTranslate backup. Start an empty web novel project and paste or import chapters as they arrive.

02

Work chapter by chapter

Compare source and translation side by side, edit the result directly, preserve partial work, and resume from your library.

03

Keep terms consistent

Maintain names and preferred terms in a project glossary. Use selectable Japanese source text with Yomitan for closer reading.

04

Finish as a book

Read the source or translation in the built-in reader, then export completed work as TXT or rebuild it as an EPUB.

Japanese at the center, with deliberate routes outward.

The primary route is Japanese to English. DuskTranslate also supports Japanese into Vietnamese, Russian, German, and Spanish, plus English into those four languages. The product does not claim universal language support: each route is introduced intentionally so prompts, interface labels, and quality checks stay understandable.

Choose the model, keep control of the work.

Use your own key from Google AI Studio, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Claude, or a compatible OpenAI-style endpoint. Provider keys stay in the editor session by default. On a trusted personal device, you can explicitly remember the key and URL for 30 days.

DuskTranslate supplies a translation-focused prompt, but the translation remains editable. Teams plans can replace the master prompt and carry shared or personal instructions into the project.

Solo library or staged team review.

Save projects locally without an account or sign in for a private cloud library. Team projects can assign Translators, Proof-readers, Quality Checkers, and a Lead, then move chapter submissions through revision and approval before publishing the final version.

Translate→Proof-read→Quality check→Lead approval
Human review still matters. AI output can be inaccurate. DuskTranslate is designed to make comparison, correction, and consistency easier, not to represent machine output as a finished human translation.

Start without committing your library to the cloud.

Open the local library, import a book, and test the workflow without creating an account. When you want cross-session cloud projects or collaboration, sign in and choose the storage that fits the project.
