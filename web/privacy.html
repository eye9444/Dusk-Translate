This policy applies to DuskTranslate, including www.dusktranslate.com, its account, project, reader, and billing features. It does not govern independent services such as AI providers, Google, Supabase, Vercel, or Paddle. Review those providers' policies before using their services.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to operate and secure the service; provide local and cloud projects, collaboration, exports, subscriptions, and support; prevent abuse; and meet legal obligations. Where a legal basis is required, processing is generally necessary to provide the requested service, for legitimate interests such as security and service reliability, with consent where required, or to meet legal obligations.

4. Public Reader Links

An active public reader link can be opened by anyone who has its token. Do not enable a reader link for material you want to keep private. Reader comments require sign-in, do not grant editor access, and may remain visible as an earlier-version comment if the linked text later changes. A project owner can revoke a link or disable new reader comments.

5. Service Providers

Supabase provides authentication and cloud data storage. Vercel hosts the application and processes ordinary infrastructure request data. Paddle processes checkout, payment method updates, subscription management, invoices, and related payment information for paid plans. Google may provide optional OAuth and hosted fonts. Translation requests go directly to the selected provider, which may include OpenRouter or Google Generative Language services. The optional Yomitan link opens an independent extension site; DuskTranslate does not receive its lookup history.

6. Storage Technologies

DuskTranslate does not intentionally use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or tracking pixels. It uses necessary browser storage: IndexedDB for local projects; localStorage for preferences and remembered authentication; and sessionStorage for tab-scoped settings. See the Cookie Policy for details.

7. Retention and Deletion

Local projects remain until you delete them, clear site data, or remove the browser profile. Cloud projects and account data remain while your account is active or as needed for the service, security, dispute handling, and legal obligations. You may delete cloud projects in the library. To request account deletion or another privacy request, email cassinipioneer@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity and may retain limited information where required by law or to resolve disputes. Third-party retention is governed by the relevant provider.

8. International Use and Transfers

DuskTranslate is operated from India and is available worldwide. Information may be processed in India and in the locations used by our service providers. By using the service, you understand that data may be transferred to and processed in those locations, subject to applicable law.

9. Your Choices and Rights

You may use the device-only library without an account, choose not to remember a session, delete local projects, download backups, delete cloud projects, manage a paid subscription through the Paddle portal, and contact us to request access, correction, export, deletion, objection, restriction, or other rights available under applicable law.

10. Security

We use HTTPS for the hosted service and access controls for cloud data. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Protect your account, use provider spending limits where available, rotate an exposed AI key promptly, and avoid sharing private books through a public reader link.

11. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy by publishing a revised version and date. For privacy questions or requests, contact cassinipioneer@gmail.com.
