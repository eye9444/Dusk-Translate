# Proposed What's New

Version: To be chosen before release.

Title: More languages. Fewer dropdown rituals.

Hey everyone. Korean and Simplified Chinese are joining the translation room. Japanese-to-English is still at the heart of DuskTranslate, but more of your reading pile can now come along.

- Translate Japanese or English into Korean or Simplified Chinese, or translate either one back into English.
- Pick your usual language pair in Account preferences. New projects will start with that choice, and signed-in preferences follow your account. New accounts get asked once they sign in. Apparently remembering two dropdowns was technology we had access to all along.
- Click a library card or the Continue bar to open your project. On touchscreens, double-tap to open; tap a gallery cover once to reveal its actions. The buttons still work with one tap, because even I have limits.

Thanks for using the site and telling me when something feels off. The books are enough work without the interface auditioning for the role of antagonist.

Eye, creator of DuskTranslate
